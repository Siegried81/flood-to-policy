July 2021 flood map

One interactive Leaflet map of the July 2021 floods in the Belgium–Germany–Netherlands border region, from exposure to planning.

Open the map (four scenes)

A single page that walks from what the satellites saw to where to act first. The scene buttons switch layers, the result panel and the legend; the map view and the planning scenario stay put.

  1. What happened? — observed Copernicus EMS flooded areas and traces.
  2. Who was exposed? — estimated residents per 400 m hexagon.
  3. Where could they go? — candidate shelters and an exploratory capacity scenario.
  4. Where to start? — a scenario-based planning priority per hexagon.
Team 9's work. The priority map is now scene 4 of this single map; the former separate priority page has been retired. The rest of this repository is my own.
