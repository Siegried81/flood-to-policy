# Q2 desk research: official sources on flood defence and policy follow-up since July 2021

Compiled 2026-10-26, extended 2026-10-29, for Q2 of the case (July 2021 floods in Belgium, Germany, the Netherlands): official sources with link, date and what they cover. **VERIFIED** means the URL was opened and the summary comes from the document; **COULD-NOT-VERIFY** and the leads at the end must be opened by hand before being cited. Press is not an official source: use it to point to a document or label it as press. Figures are as the source prints them. Several documents are also in the repo (`config/sources.yaml`, `data/raw/scrape/`).

Hub page for the Walloon documents: <https://www.wallonie.be/fr/inondations-de-juillet-2021-rapports-etudes-projets-subventions>.
